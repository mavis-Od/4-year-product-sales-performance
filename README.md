# 4-Year Product Sales Performance Dashboard (2019–2022)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes product sales performance over four years, from 2019 to 2022, using Microsoft Power BI and Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales volume, revenue, costs, gross profit, profit margin, product performance, and monthly sales trends to support business decision-making.

## Business Problem

Businesses need to understand which products generate the most revenue and profit, how sales change over time, and whether their sales performance is meeting business objectives.

This project uses historical sales data to answer these questions and present the results in an interactive dashboard.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Compare revenue and gross profit across products.
- Identify monthly sales trends and changes in performance.
- Examine sales volume across different products.
- Allow users to filter results by year from 2019 to 2022.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel — data storage and preparation.
- Microsoft Power BI — data modeling, KPI reporting, and dashboard development.
- DAX — calculation of business measures and KPIs.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes:

- Total Units Sold
- Total Cost
- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Average Profit per Unit
- Average Selling Price
- Profit Margin

## Dashboard Features

- Monthly comparison of gross profit and total revenue.
- Gross profit by product.
- Total revenue by product.
- Total units sold by product.
- Interactive year slicer for 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022.

## Initial Observations

Based on the dashboard visualization:

- Samsung 46-inch television appears to lead the displayed products in revenue and gross profit.
- Nestle Coffee appears to have the highest total units sold among the displayed products.
- Monthly revenue varies across the year, indicating changes in sales performance over time.
- The year slicer allows users to investigate performance across the four-year period.

These observations should be confirmed against the underlying dataset and measures before being treated as final business findings.

## Business Recommendations

- Review the sales and profitability of leading products to understand what drives their performance.
- Investigate products with lower revenue or profit to identify opportunities for pricing, promotion, or sales improvement.
- Monitor monthly performance to identify strong and weak sales periods.
- Compare annual results to understand changes in product performance over time.
- Use both sales volume and profitability when making product decisions rather than relying on revenue alone.
